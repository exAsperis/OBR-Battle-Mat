Simple scene control

Choose the background once. Let every player see it.

Select a built-in background or one of your own OBR maps, then choose a render distance that suits each device. The background remains shared while every player controls their own performance.

Strictly bounded

"Off" through "Extreme" presets cap the pool of rendered tiles. Panning recycles tiles, and zooming out never creates more tiles.
